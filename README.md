# 🩺 AI_Doctor: Your Virtual Health Assistant

Welcome to **AI_Doctor**, a cutting-edge virtual health assistant designed to provide personalized medical information and guidance. By leveraging advanced AI models, AI_Doctor offers accurate and timely health advice, making healthcare more accessible and efficient.

---

## 🔍 Overview

AI_Doctor utilizes state-of-the-art AI models to analyze user inputs and provide relevant medical information. Whether you're seeking information on symptoms, medications, or general health advice, AI_Doctor is here to assist you.

---

## 🧠 Features

- **Symptom Analysis**: Input your symptoms and receive potential diagnoses.
- **Medication Information**: Get details about various medications, including uses and side effects.
- **Health Tips**: Receive personalized health tips based on your inputs.
